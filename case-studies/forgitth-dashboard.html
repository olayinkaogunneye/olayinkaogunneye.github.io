Project Overview

Forgitth Pharmaceuticals required a clear, executive-ready view of sales and marketing performance. I designed and built a Power BI dashboard that brings together revenue, product, region, and campaign data into a single, interactive analytics experience.

Problem

Commercial performance data was fragmented across multiple sources, making it difficult to:

  • Track revenue by product and region
  • Measure marketing campaign effectiveness
  • Understand sales trends over time
  • Provide executives with a single source of truth

Reporting was manual, slow, and prone to inconsistencies.

Solution

I built a complete Power BI solution with:

  • Power Query for data ingestion and transformation
  • A star-schema data model for sales and marketing
  • DAX measures for revenue, growth, and performance KPIs
  • Interactive visuals for executives and managers

The result is a clean, reliable, and interactive performance dashboard.

Data Model & Architecture

The solution uses a star-schema model:

  • Fact Sales — Transaction-level sales data
  • Dim Product — Product attributes and categories
  • Dim Customer / Region — Customer and geographic segmentation
  • Dim Date — Calendar and fiscal periods
  • Dim Campaign — Marketing initiatives and spend

Tools used:

  • Power BI
  • Power Query
  • DAX
  • Excel / CSV source files

Impact

  • Single source of truth for sales and marketing performance
  • Clear visibility into top-performing products and regions
  • Improved understanding of campaign ROI
  • Faster, more reliable reporting for executives

Outcome

The Forgitth Pharmaceuticals dashboard provides:

  • Executive-ready views of revenue and growth
  • Drill-down capabilities for product and region analysis
  • Integrated sales and marketing performance insights
  • A foundation for future analytics expansion
